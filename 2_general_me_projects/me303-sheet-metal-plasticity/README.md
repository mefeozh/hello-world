## 📌 Technical Summary

Models sheet metal plastic deformation, springback ratio, and press brake punch force during V-die air bending:
- Neutral axis plastic arc length and gentle bend radius threshold ($R_b/h > 4$).
- Elastic springback ratio: $R_b/R_f = 1 - 3k + 4k^3$, where $k = (R_b/h)(Y/E)$.
- Punch tonnage prediction: $F_b = \frac{K w h^2 \text{UTS}}{w_{\text{die}}}$.
