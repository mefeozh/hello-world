# 🔄 ME 301: Planar Mechanism Kinematics & Dynamic Force Equilibrium

**Author:** Mehmet Efe Özhan (METU Mechanical Engineering)

---

## 📌 Technical Summary

Solves analytical kinematics and dynamic force equilibrium for 4-bar linkages, slider-cranks, and inverted slider-cranks:
- **Vector-Loop Closure:** Analytical position, angular velocity, and angular acceleration via Freudenstein's equation.
- **Inverse Dynamics:** D'Alembert dynamic joint reactions and required input driving torque over full crank rotations ($0^\circ - 360^\circ$) under velocity-dependent shaping loads.
