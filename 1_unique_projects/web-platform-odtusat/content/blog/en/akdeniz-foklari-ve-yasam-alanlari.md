<h1 style="font-size:32px;margin-bottom:20px;">Mediterranean Monk Seals and Their Habitats</h1>

<img src="/assets/images/banners/afag-banner.avif"
alt="Mediterranean monk seal on the water surface"
style="width:100%;height:200px;border-radius:8px;margin:20px 0;object-fit:cover;">

<p>
The <strong>Mediterranean monk seal</strong> (Monachus monachus) is one of the rarest and most endangered marine mammals on Earth. 
Today, their population worldwide is known to be critically low. 
If we want to protect these unique creatures and nature, getting to know them closely is of great importance.
<em>Proper conservation policies, habitat protection, and public awareness</em> are critical factors for the survival of this species.
</p>

<p>
In the past, these creatures roamed freely along all the coasts of the Mediterranean and the Black Sea, but today they can only survive in specific isolated regions. 
These areas generally consist of steep cliffs and <strong>coastal caves</strong> that are difficult for humans to access. These caves are vital as breeding and resting grounds.
</p>

<img src="/assets/images/banners/afag-banner.avif"
alt="Coastal cave and rocky area"
style="width:320px;height:auto;float:right;margin:10px 0 15px 20px;border-radius:8px;">

<p>
The habitats of Mediterranean monk seals are gradually shrinking. Human-induced factors such as coastal development, overfishing, and marine pollution make life difficult for these delicate creatures. 
Their primary food sources, <strong>fish and octopuses</strong>, are equally threatened. 
<em>Human interference</em> can even cause pups to be abandoned in their caves.
</p>

<p>
When encountering a Mediterranean monk seal in the wild, the best course of action is to stay calm and observe from a distance. 
Approaching or trying to feed them can cause them immense stress. 
Therefore, our most fundamental rule is <strong>to keep our distance and not disturb them</strong>.
</p>

<div style="clear:both;"></div>

<h2 style="margin-top:30px;">Rules for Protecting Monk Seals</h2>

<ul style="padding-left:20px;">
<li><strong>Do not enter caves.</strong> Stay away from the seals' breeding and resting areas.</li>
<li><em>Be quiet</em> and avoid sudden movements if you encounter one.</li>
<li>Never try to feed or touch them.</li>
<li>Do not leave your <strong>waste</strong> in nature to prevent marine pollution.</li>
<li>Report any injured or distressed seals you see to authorized institutions.</li>
</ul>

<h2 style="margin-top:30px;">Hope for the Future</h2>

<p>
With the raised awareness of locals and sailors, there are slight signs of recovery in the Mediterranean monk seal population. 
Protected marine parks, in particular, serve as safe havens for these animals.
Protecting Mediterranean monk seals, which play an indispensable role in the <strong>balance of the marine ecosystem</strong>, means protecting not just one species, but all our seas.
</p>
