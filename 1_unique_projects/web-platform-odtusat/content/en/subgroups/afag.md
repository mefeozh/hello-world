<h3 class="block-title">About Us</h3>
<p class="block-text"><strong>Founded in 1987 under ODTU-SAT.</strong></p>

<h4 style="margin-top: 20px; margin-bottom: 10px; font-weight: 600;">Our Goals:</h4>
<ul class="info-list">
<li>To conduct research in aquatic ecosystems and work for the continuity of their natural structures.</li>
<li>To bridge the scientific knowledge gap regarding the biology, ecology, and behavior of the Mediterranean monk seal and other flagship species.</li>
<li>To raise public awareness, especially those living in coastal areas, and include them in conservation efforts.</li>
<li>To establish responsible fishing awareness and work towards sustainable fishing practices in Turkey.</li>
<li>To cooperate with other relevant institutions and organizations in line with its vision and mission.</li>
</ul>

<hr style="border: 0; border-top: 1px solid #eee; margin: 30px 0;">

<p class="block-text"><strong>AFAG Vision:</strong> Sustainable nature conservation in the light of global thinking and local conditions.</p>
<p class="block-text"><strong>AFAG Mission:</strong> To ensure the sustainable protection of all marine, coastal, and freshwater ecosystems of Turkey, free from all threats, in accordance with all laws, rules, and ethics, including the human factor.</p>
</div>

<h3 class="block-title">About Mediterranean Monk Seal</h3>

<div class="image-block">
<picture>
<source srcset="/assets/images/afag-morphology.avif" type="image/avif">
<img src="/assets/images/afag-morphology.jpg" alt="Mediterranean Monk Seal Morphology">
</picture>
<div class="image-caption">Mediterranean Monk Seal (Monachus monachus)</div>
</div>

<p class="block-text">They are 2-3 meters long and weigh between 200-300 kg. Adults are covered with 0.5 cm short and stiff hairs.</p>

<div class="seal-info">
<h4>Morphology</h4>
<p><strong>Male:</strong> Dark brown close to black. There is a distinct white patch on the belly.</p>
<br>
<p><strong>Female:</strong> Light brown or gray tones. Their bellies are lighter in color.</p>
<br>
<p><strong>Pup:</strong> About 80-90 cm and 20 kg at birth. There is a white patch on the belly. Its body is covered with shiny black hairs.</p>
</div>

<p class="block-text"><strong>Feeding:</strong> Carnivorous. It dives to hunt creatures such as fish, octopus, and lobster underwater.</p>
<p class="block-text"><strong>Behavior:</strong> Less social than other pinnipeds. They usually roam alone. They give birth in coastal caves not visited by humans.</p>
</div>

<!-- Threats Block -->
<div class="content-block">
<h3 class="block-title">Reasons for Extinction</h3>
<ul class="info-list">
<li><strong>Invasion of Habitats:</strong> Roads opening to natural coasts, second homes and tourist facilities.</li>
<li><strong>Illegal and Overfishing:</strong> Depletion of food sources.</li>
<li><strong>Deliberate Killing and Accidental Deaths:</strong> Entanglement in nets and fisherman-seal competition.</li>
<li><strong>Tourist Dives to Seal Caves:</strong> Human pressure causing seals to abandon their habitats.</li>
<li><strong>Marine and Coastal Pollution:</strong> Degradation of habitats.</li>
</ul>
</div>
