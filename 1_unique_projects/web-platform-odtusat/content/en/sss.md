<!-- Q1 -->
<div class="accordion-item">
    <h3 class="accordion-header" role="button" aria-expanded="false" tabindex="0">1. How to become a member of ODTU-SAT?</h3>
    <div class="accordion-content" role="region">
        Among the candidates applying to become a member by joining METU Subaqua Society and taking Basic Diving
        Training, a certain number of candidates will be entitled to receive basic diving training according to the
        ranking formed as a result of the evaluation made out of 100 points. The evaluation consists of the written
        exam score covering the theoretical knowledge questions explained in SAT-101 lessons and general ODTU-SAT
        questions, and the activity score showing the candidates' interest and participation status within the
        community. For detailed information see:
        <a href="{{ i18n "uyelik_slug" | relLangURL }}" style="color: var(--primary-color); text-decoration: underline;">Membership System</a>
    </div>
</div>

<!-- Q2 -->
<div class="accordion-item">
    <h3 class="accordion-header" role="button" aria-expanded="false" tabindex="0">2. Are non-METU members accepted to ODTU-SAT?</h3>
    <div class="accordion-content" role="region">
        According to the regulations of the METU Sports Directorate, only METU students are accepted.
    </div>
</div>

<!-- Q3 -->
<div class="accordion-item">
    <h3 class="accordion-header" role="button" aria-expanded="false" tabindex="0">3. Is there a membership fee for ODTU-SAT?</h3>
    <div class="accordion-content" role="region">
        No dues are collected from ODTU-SAT members. However, a one-off small amount of money is collected from
        candidate members to cover stationery expenses.
    </div>
</div>

<!-- Q4 -->
<div class="accordion-item">
    <h3 class="accordion-header" role="button" aria-expanded="false" tabindex="0">4. Can you give information about the Basic Training Camp?</h3>
    <div class="accordion-content" role="region">
        For SAT 102 – Basic Diving Training Practices, two camps, each lasting one week, are organized in Kaş,
        Antalya during the semester break (in February). Candidate members attend one of these two camps according
        to their preferences. The bus ticket and accommodation are arranged by the community. The camp cost is
        divided among the individuals (travel, food, accommodation, diving).
    </div>
</div>

<!-- Q5 -->
<div class="accordion-item">
    <h3 class="accordion-header" role="button" aria-expanded="false" tabindex="0">5. How is diving equipment supplied?</h3>
    <div class="accordion-content" role="region">
        You can either provide diving equipment such as ABC (mask, fins, snorkel), neoprene suit, booties yourself
        or you can complete your camp by borrowing from community members before going to the training camp (which
        many new members do). You can obtain your own personal items later, and you won't have equipment problems
        before camps. What you should pay attention to when buying diving equipment and where you can find it will
        be explained to you before the camps. Diving equipment such as tanks, BCDs, regulators are available in
        the community. There is a certain rental fee for them within the community itself.
    </div>
</div>

<!-- Q6 -->
<div class="accordion-item">
    <h3 class="accordion-header" role="button" aria-expanded="false" tabindex="0">6. Do you have pool or land trainings?</h3>
    <div class="accordion-content" role="region">
        As free diving trainings, pool trainings are held in METU pool for beginners to get used to water and
        move comfortably in water, and for advanced levels for development and conditioning. Land trainings are
        held to gain and maintain conditioning.
    </div>
</div>

<!-- Q7 -->
<div class="accordion-item">
    <h3 class="accordion-header" role="button" aria-expanded="false" tabindex="0">7. What happens in sub-group meetings? Is attendance mandatory?</h3>
    <div class="accordion-content" role="region">
        First, agenda items are determined. Topics such as projects/activities to be done, lessons to be opened
        are discussed. Then, those who are available go to eat. Attendance at meetings is not mandatory.
    </div>
</div>

<!-- Q8 -->
<div class="accordion-item">
    <h3 class="accordion-header" role="button" aria-expanded="false" tabindex="0">8. Can you inform about your training system? Do lessons continue after basic training camp?</h3>
    <div class="accordion-content" role="region">
        Yes. For our members who complete Basic Diving Training Theory (SAT 101) and Basic Diving Training
        Practices (SAT 102) courses, there is a curriculum they can follow to improve their diving skills and
        techniques; to become a comprehensive research diver in theory and practice. Apart from these, each
        sub-group will have its own lessons to be opened.
    </div>
</div>

<!-- Q9 -->
<div class="accordion-item">
    <h3 class="accordion-header" role="button" aria-expanded="false" tabindex="0">9. Who or which sub-group gives the basic diving training? Do I need to follow sub-groups for basic training?</h3>
    <div class="accordion-content" role="region">
        You can think of the Basic Diving Training theoretical course as an introduction to diving course,
        independent of sub-groups. It is given by our members who have a certain accumulation of knowledge.
        Sub-groups work on their own fields of interest, produce camps and projects. Since most of the activities
        to be held by METU SAT will be research-oriented and goal-oriented, you are expected to follow the
        sub-groups. Your participation in sub-groups will show us how active you are. You can get more detailed
        information at sub-group introductory meetings.
    </div>
</div>

<!-- Q10 -->
<div class="accordion-item">
    <h3 class="accordion-header" role="button" aria-expanded="false" tabindex="0">10. Are sub-groups independent of each other, can I follow different groups?</h3>
    <div class="accordion-content" role="region">
        When sub-groups are presented separately, people thought they were disconnected/independent from each
        other. Sub-groups are not independent of each other. Many friends follow more than one sub-group. You can
        follow the sub-groups you want as much as your interest and time allow. Meeting times will be different,
        activities/diving days will be different as much as possible.
    </div>
</div>

<!-- Q11 -->
<div class="accordion-item">
    <h3 class="accordion-header" role="button" aria-expanded="false" tabindex="0">11. Do I need to attend classes for the new term, do I need to pay the first fee taken from candidate members?</h3>
    <div class="accordion-content" role="region">
        Candidate members who followed the lessons last term and passed the exam and interview do not need to pay
        the first money. However, they need to refresh their knowledge and take the exam again; for this reason,
        they should come to the lessons again.
    </div>
</div>

<!-- Q12 -->
<div class="accordion-item">
    <h3 class="accordion-header" role="button" aria-expanded="false" tabindex="0">12. What is the bylaws mentioned in the membership form, where can I find it?</h3>
    <div class="accordion-content" role="region">
        Bylaws; is a set of rules prepared to explain the order and functioning of METU Subaqua Society.
        <br><br>
        You can reach our <a href="{{ i18n "tuzuk_slug" | relLangURL }}" style="color: var(--primary-color); text-decoration: underline;">bylaws</a> from the documents on our site.
    </div>
</div>

<!-- Q13 -->
<div class="accordion-item">
    <h3 class="accordion-header" role="button" aria-expanded="false" tabindex="0">13. I have dived before, got my badge. Do you recognize this, do you have an equivalency system?</h3>
    <div class="accordion-content" role="region">
        We accept transitions from systems with a solid foundation such as PADI, CMAS, BSAC, NAUI, whose training
        standards are determined, as equivalent to ODTU-SAT training from a lower level. For example, new divers
        with 1* CMAS or PADI Open Water Diver badges are required to come to ODTU-SAT basic training. Divers who
        have received higher level training are considered to have completed the training at a lower level in
        ODTU-SAT training. These people do not need to come to basic training or take the exam; however, they are
        expected to actively follow the community for a semester. The logbooks and badges of these candidates must
        be submitted to the Board of Directors or Technical Committee members together with their originals and
        photocopies for evaluation.
    </div>
</div>

<!-- Q14 -->
<div class="accordion-item">
    <h3 class="accordion-header" role="button" aria-expanded="false" tabindex="0">14. Are there conditions like being a good swimmer to join the Free Diving Group?</h3>
    <div class="accordion-content" role="region">
        FDG has regular trainings during the semester. For this reason, eliminations are held at the beginning of
        the semester to select people who can keep up with this tempo and are believed to continue with the group.
        Introductory meetings, FDG first meetings are important to create a list of people who will enter the
        eliminations or just want to swim regularly, they should be followed.
    </div>
</div>

<!-- Q15 -->
<div class="accordion-item">
    <h3 class="accordion-header" role="button" aria-expanded="false" tabindex="0">15. How often do you go diving, when are project camps held?</h3>
    <div class="accordion-content" role="region">
        People who have received basic training and become ODTU-SAT members can organize activities for many
        purposes such as increasing their diving experience, conducting research and examination, etc. It is enough
        for people who can fulfill their responsibilities to come together for the realization of activities carried
        out entirely with voluntary work and effort. In this way, an activity can be organized every weekend, every
        day when free time can be found. Project camps are usually held in the summer months or during the semester
        break as they will be detailed, planned camps that will last longer.
    </div>
</div>
