<div class="content-block">
    <h3 class="block-title">Establishment and Purpose</h3>
    <p class="block-text">
        METU Subaqua Society was founded in 1985 by a group of METU students and instructors as a student
        community affiliated with the Sports Directorate under the METU Rectorate.
    </p>
    <p class="block-text">
        The fundamental principle adopted by ODTU-SAT at its establishment is the research, protection, and
        promotion of historical, cultural, and natural values in Turkey's coasts and inland waters.
        Activities carried out in this context include providing information and skills to community members
        by giving basic and advanced diving, search, recovery, and first aid training; raising divers for
        exploration and examination studies; conducting research and organizing promotional activities (such
        as competitions, panels, meetings, seminars) on ecological values (such as Seagrasses, Sea Turtles,
        Mediterranean Monk Seal), natural values (such as underwater caves, offshore rocks, and marine life
        shelters), and cultural values (shipwrecks, underwater archaeology, maritime history) in our coasts
        and inland waters.
    </p>
</div>

<div class="content-block">
    <h3 class="block-title">Projects from Past to Present</h3>
    <p class="block-text">
        The community conducted a basic research study in Dalyan, the breeding beach of sea turtle species,
        in 1986/1987. The Mediterranean Monk Seal Conservation Project also emerged from within the METU
        Subaqua Society in those years and has survived to this day.
    </p>
    <p class="block-text">
        Efforts to find and identify the Ottoman and Russian Command Ships sunk in the Ottoman-Russian Naval
        Battle in Çeşme Bay (1988-1990), two 1600-year-old shipwrecks and glass ingots found in each
        (1992/1993) on the Antakya coast, which were also found by the community, are the firsts of METU
        Subaqua Society's contributions to the science of Archaeology.
    </p>
    <p class="block-text">
        In addition, "Underwater Image Hunting Competition" SAGAY, organized by the community for the first
        time in Bozcaada in 1992, aimed to ensure the establishment and operation of "marine parks" in the
        North Aegean by offering a meaningful option such as Image Hunting with a Camera instead of
        Spearfishing, as well as being the first underwater photography competition organized in Turkey.
    </p>
    <p class="block-text">
        Recently, the community has been carrying out important projects such as Turkey Coasts Sea Caves
        Inventory Project, Cilicia Region Archaeological Survey, Investigation of Seagrass Distribution,
        Uluburun III Artificial Reef Project, and Free Diving Trials.
    </p>
</div>

<div class="content-block">
    <h3 class="block-title">Structure and Functioning</h3>
    <p class="block-text">
        METU Subaqua Society is responsible to the Rectorate. The executive board consisting of the Academic
        Advisor and five students, and the supervisory board consisting of three students are responsible
        for ensuring the operation. The Executive Board and the Supervisory Board are responsible to the
        General Assembly.
    </p>
    <p class="block-text">
        The community has various sub-groups and working units such as Mediterranean Monk Seal Research
        Group (MSRG), Wreck Research Group (WRG), Ecology Group (ECOG), Photography and Videography
        Group (PVRG), Cave Diving and Research Group (CDRG) and Free Diving Group (FDG).
    </p>
</div>
